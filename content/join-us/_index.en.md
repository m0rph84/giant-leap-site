## Recruitment

If you want to join Giant Leap, the goal is not just to add another name to the list. We are looking for people who actually want to take part in the life of the org.

## How to join

1. Reach out through our official channels.
2. Introduce yourself and tell us what kind of Star Citizen experience you are looking for.
3. Talk to the staff so we can align on fit, availability, and interests.

## What we look for

- A collaborative mindset
- Respectful and reliable behavior
- Interest in org activity and teamwork
- The will to help build something that lasts

## Contact

For applications, collaborations, or questions you can use the social channels on the homepage or the org Discord server.

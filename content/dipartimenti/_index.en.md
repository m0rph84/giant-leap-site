## Org departments

This section is meant to describe the active departments of Giant Leap and the role each one plays inside the org.

## What belongs here

- **Exploration**: scouting, reconnaissance, and mapping
- **Industry**: resource gathering, refining, and production
- **Logistics**: transport, operational support, and cargo coordination
- **Security**: escort, defense, and protection of org activities

## Goal

This page can grow into an organizational hub with responsibilities, contacts, and typical activities for each department.

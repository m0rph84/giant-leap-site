## Our direction

Giant Leap is an Italian **Star Citizen** org built around teamwork, reliability, and the ambition to grow together over time.

We are not just trying to fill a roster. We want to build a group that can organize itself properly, welcome new pilots, and give continuity to the org's activity.

## What we want to build

- Coordinated group operations that stay accessible
- A mature and orderly environment without drama
- Support for new members who want to learn
- Events, content, and community moments beyond the single login session

## Our values

- **Respect** in communication and in-game behavior
- **Consistency** over noise
- **Collaboration** before ego
- **Clarity** about roles, goals, and expectations
